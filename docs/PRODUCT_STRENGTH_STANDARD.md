# GRADD PRODUCT STRENGTH STANDARD — every paper, the same bar
**Adopted 14/07/2026 (Grant). Paper-agnostic. Every current and future subject/paper — ACCA papers, IB, LC, anything after — must meet this standard before its tier claims are made. The AFM Coverage Contract is the reference implementation; every new paper starts by instantiating its own coverage contract from the official syllabus, to this template. No paper ships to a weaker bar because it came later.**

## The principle
Product strength = the surface area of things that actually move a student's grade. Not features, not engagement mechanics, not content volume — verified, exam-faithful practice and teaching that converts a weak answer into a scoring one. Fluff and games are explicitly out of scope (per GRADD_PRODUCT_MODEL: heavy gamification/streaks = skip). If an artifact doesn't move the dial on exam day, it doesn't count toward strength.

## Non-negotiables — every paper, before any public claim

1. **A coverage contract, from the official syllabus.** Exact LO extraction → calculator families / narrative clusters → drill counts → tier definitions (demo / section-live / viable paid launch / exam-ready), with the exam's own structure (mark split, guaranteed sections, professional-skills marks) as the load-bearing facts. No estimates left standing: the contract is a checklist, not a judgement.

2. **Code owns every figure and every verdict.** Quantitative drills: deterministic calculator owns all numbers and all figure-vs-figure decisions (accept/reject, rankings, flips); the model authors prose only, and never states a number or an inequality the code didn't produce. Graded chains carry own-figure-rule credit; tolerances set to examiner-fidelity, not convenience.

3. **The full verification pipeline, no shortcuts on later papers:** generate → all gates (incl. the accumulated pattern gates: loss-relief, figure-integrity at all displayed precisions, distinct-factor OFR carry, template hygiene, completeness demands) → independent adjudicator recompute → blind external hostile review (fresh reviewer, no shared context, CLOSED-RULINGS section) → adjudication with journalled accepts/rejects → flip by explicit-id reviewed SQL with reconcile-before-flip. Both batches of review evidence journalled.

4. **Exam-format practice at the top of the ladder.** Mocks built to the paper's real structure (sections, mark splits, professional-skills marks), generated by the exam-rehearsal engine — never hand-authored ad hoc. Exam-ready claims require them.

5. **Honest claims, tier-gated (selling bible binds).** Coverage claims match shipped counts exactly; "exam-ready"/"complete" only at the exam-ready tier; early-access framing below it. The landing surface states what's live, numerically.

6. **Teaching, not just marking.** Every drill carries the teach-through design: diagnosis of the real misconception (named failure classes per paper), hint that points at the thinking gap without leaking, verbatim code-verified reveal, evaluation that takes a position (no fence-sitting). Marking alone is commodity; the taught layer is the product.

7. **Evidence loop.** Per paper: progress/weak-area tracking wired from day one, transcripts persisted, attribution on signups — so teaching quality becomes measurable and pass-rate proof can eventually be claimed honestly.

## What does NOT count toward strength
Gamification, streaks, badges, engagement mechanics for their own sake; unverified generated content in any student-visible surface; breadth claims ahead of shipped counts; features that demo well but don't change what a student writes in the exam hall.

## Standing test for any proposed feature or content item
"Does this change what the student writes (or how they allocate time) in the exam hall?" If no — it queues behind everything that does.
